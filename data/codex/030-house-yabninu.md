The name is real: Yabninu's archive was excavated from the ruins of Ugarit in the reader's history, ledgers and letters belonging to a man who actually lived. Here, the city never burned, the house was never buried, and the descendants still hold the books.

Chartered since the six-twenties, House Yabninu is new money by the standards of institutions six centuries old — aggressive, unsentimental, and the first great house to fund a press outright. Yabninu bets on road script, cheap reproduction, and volume, and it is usually right.

It is the natural patron for a minor house with more ambition than standing. Yabninu will back you generously, promptly, and exactly as long as backing you pays.
